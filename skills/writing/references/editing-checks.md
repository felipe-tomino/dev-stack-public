# Editing checks

Use after substantial drafting or rewriting. [editing.md](editing.md) owns the principles and named patterns; this is a final check, not a second editing procedure. Fix failures before returning the result; do not produce a checklist report unless requested.

1. Compare with the source: are meaning, specific facts, uncertainty and attribution intact, without invented claims?
2. Read aloud: would the writer recognize their vocabulary, cadence, humor and useful edge? Undo edits that merely make strong sentences uniform or strip character.
3. Check clarity: does the reader get the point without unnecessary setup, tangled sentences or lost nuance?
4. Scan for the words and patterns in the editing reference, including inflated claims, robotic rhythm, decorative formatting and artificial endings. Preserve intentional voice rather than polishing mechanically.
5. Match the requested output: return the draft/edit without an unsolicited change report. For critique/detect requests, quote the patterns found and suggest concise fixes; do not rewrite, score or claim AI authorship.
